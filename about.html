About Hydra

We believe hydration changes everything.

Hydra was born from a simple truth — most people are chronically dehydrated and don't even know it. We built the simplest, most beautiful way to fix that, one sip at a time.

Brain Function
Even mild dehydration (1–2%) can impair concentration, short-term memory, and reaction time.
⚡
Energy Levels
Fatigue is one of the first signs of dehydration. Drinking enough water can boost daily energy by up to 30%.
💪
Physical Performance
Athletes can lose 6–10% of body weight through sweat. Hydration is the most underrated performance lever.
🌿
Long-Term Health
Consistent hydration supports kidney health, skin quality, digestion, and joint lubrication over time.
What's Inside

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Hydra is designed with one goal: remove every possible reason not to track your water intake.

💧
One-tap logging
Pre-set amounts from espresso shots to litre bottles. Log a sip in under a second.
📊
Live progress
An animated bottle fills as you drink. Visual feedback makes the habit stick.
🔔
Smart reminders
Set custom intervals and active hours. Gentle nudges, not annoying spam.
🎯
Personal targets
Set your daily goal anywhere from 500ml to 5L based on your body and lifestyle.
🔥
Streak tracking
Hit your goal daily and build a streak. Consistency is the whole game.
🔒
Private by design
All data lives on your device. No account, no server, no tracking. Just you and your water.
How It Works

Simple enough to actually use.

1
Set your daily target
Go to Settings and drag the slider to your goal — from 500ml to 5L. The WHO recommends 2000–2500ml for most adults.
2
Log every sip
Tap a quick-add button — coffee, glass, bottle — or type a custom amount. The bottle fills in real time.
3
Set a reminder
Enable drink reminders with your interval and active hours. A gentle chime and popup will nudge you throughout the day.
4
Build the habit
Hit your goal daily to grow your streak. Come back tomorrow. That's all there is to it.
